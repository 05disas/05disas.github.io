VFS DBVF Award-Winning VTOL Aircraft

Georgia Tech Design Build Vertical Fly competition aircraft with autonomous mission and payload mechanism work.

Overview

The Georgia Tech DBVF team designs and builds a vertical flight aircraft for the Vertical Flight Society Design-Build-Vertical-Fly competition. In 2026, the team placed 1st overall.

My Role

I led the 20+ member team as Chief Engineer for the 2026 competition and am serving as Vice President for the 2027 competition.

Technologies Used

  • ArduPilot-based flight system.
  • RTK-GPS autonomous mission capability.
  • Arduino-controlled drop/pick mechanism.

Challenges

A major integration challenge was connecting autonomous flight behavior with a payload mechanism that could activate at specified mission waypoints.

Results / Current Status

The team placed 1st overall at the 2026 VFS DBVF competition.
