ME 2110 Competition Robot

Georgia Tech ME 2110 design competition robot focused on mechanism design, prototyping, testing, and integration.

Overview

This robot was built for Georgia Tech’s ME 2110 design competition, where teams develop, test, and iterate mechanical systems for a final performance challenge.

My Role

I contributed to mechanism design, prototyping, testing, and system integration throughout the project.

Technologies Used

  • Mechanical design and rapid prototyping.
  • Testing, iteration, and system integration.
  • Competition design review preparation.

Challenges

The project required balancing competition performance, manufacturability, mechanism reliability, and fast iteration under course deadlines.

Results / Current Status

The robot placed 3rd in the final competition and 2nd in design review.
