Overview

This research project modeled a transforming Ramblin’ Wreck-to-Buzz structure for the Georgia Tech Advanced Manufacturing Pilot Facility.

My Role

As an Undergraduate Research Assistant, I modeled the structure in SolidWorks and reduced the mass by 40%.

Technologies Used

  • SolidWorks structural modeling.
  • Design for CNC, PBF, and EDM constraints.
  • Alloy-based manufacturing strategy exploration.

Challenges

The design had to preserve geometric detail while meeting manufacturing constraints across CNC, powder bed fusion, and EDM processes.

Results / Current Status

The revised model reduced mass by 40% while supporting continued manufacturing strategy exploration.
