I’m Disas Sooriyarachchi, a Mechanical Engineering student at Georgia Tech focused on robotics, autonomy, UAVs, and intelligent mechanical systems. My work combines hands-on engineering with controls, embedded systems, flight testing, and software, from building and tuning FPV drones to leading multidisciplinary design teams and conducting research in aerial systems.

I’m especially interested in problems where mechanical design, sensing, control, and computation come together. Whether I’m developing UAV health-monitoring methods, designing robotic systems, or testing hardware in the real world, I enjoy taking projects from an initial idea through design, integration, and validation.
